DEEP DIVING INTO AN IDEA
==========================

After getting an idea, start with the following activities to explore
and understand the idea deeply.


1. BRAINSTORMING
----------------
- Generate as many ideas and possible solutions as possible.
- Encourage everyone to share their thoughts.
- Focus on quantity first rather than judging ideas.


2. DOCUMENTATION
----------------
- Record ideas, research, decisions, and findings.
- Make proper notes so important information is not lost.
- Keep references and evidence for important decisions.


3. FOCUS
--------
- Clearly define the problem you are trying to solve.
- Keep the team focused on the user's actual needs.
- Avoid getting distracted by unnecessary features.


4. DO NOT ALLOW EARLY EVALUATION
--------------------------------
- During brainstorming, do not immediately judge or reject ideas.
- First collect and explore ideas.
- Evaluate and filter the ideas after the brainstorming process.


5. GATHER A DIVERSE TEAM
------------------------
- Include people with different skills, experiences, and perspectives.
- A team may include UX designers, developers, marketers,
  business people, and users.


6. QUESTION THE OBVIOUS
-----------------------
- Do not simply accept existing assumptions.
- Ask questions such as:
  "Why?"
  "Why not?"
  "Is there a better way?"
- Challenge existing solutions and assumptions.


EVALUATING THE IDEA
===================

A good idea can be evaluated using three important criteria:


1. FEASIBLE
-----------
Question: Can we actually build or implement it?

Consider:
- Technology
- Resources
- Skills
- Time
- Cost


2. DESIRABLE
------------
Question: Do users actually want or need it?

Consider:
- User problems
- User expectations
- User research
- Usability
- Market demand


3. VIABLE
---------
Question: Can the idea work as a sustainable product or business?

Consider:
- Business model
- Revenue
- Market
- Competition
- Long-term sustainability


A strong idea should aim to be:

FEASIBLE + DESIRABLE + VIABLE


ROLE OF A UX DESIGNER
=====================

A UX designer does not work alone. They also collaborate with
other teams, including:


MARKETING
---------
- Understand customers and their needs.
- Help communicate the value of the product.


BRANDING
--------
- Maintain a consistent brand identity.
- Ensure the product experience matches the brand.


DEVELOPMENT
-----------
- Work with developers to make sure designs can be implemented.


PRODUCT AND BUSINESS TEAMS
--------------------------
- Balance user needs with business goals.
- Help define product priorities.


COMPETITOR RESEARCH
===================

1. IDENTIFY KEY COMPETITORS
---------------------------
Find products or services that solve the same or similar problem.


2. INFORM YOUR DESIGN
---------------------
Study competitors to understand:
- What they do well
- What they do poorly
- Their features
- Their user experience
- Their strengths and weaknesses


3. SOLVE USABILITY PROBLEMS
---------------------------
Use competitor research and user research to identify problems
and create a better user experience.


4. PROVIDE RELIABLE EVIDENCE
----------------------------
Do not make design decisions only based on assumptions.

Use:
- User research
- Surveys
- Interviews
- Analytics
- Competitor research
- Usability testing


5. MAKE PROPER NOTES
--------------------
Document:
- Research findings
- User problems
- Competitor observations
- Design decisions
- Evidence supporting those decisions
