A spreadsheet is a digital document that organizes data in rows and columns to help you store, calculate, and analyze information.

Where Spreadsheets Are Mainly Used

(A) Business and FinanceAccounting: 
1- Creating balance sheets, 
2- Income statements,
3- Tax tracking

(B) Budgeting: Managing company expenses and predicting future revenue.
Payroll: Calculating worker hours, wages, and tax deductions.

(C) Data Management and AnalysisInventory Tracking: 
1- Listing product stock levels
2- Costs,
3- Warehouse locations.

(D) Project Management: 
1- Building timelines, 
2- Task lists, 
3- Project deadlines
